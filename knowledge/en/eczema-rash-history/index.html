Skin Journal / Image History

Keep an image history of eczema or a recurring rash

When you want to record eczema or a recurring rash, photographs can become scattered across your camera roll. Keep the images, dates and your own notes together for the area you choose to document.

Choose a Spot. Add a Log. Return.

  1. Choose the skin area you want to record and create a Spot for it.
  2. Add a dated Log with a photograph and optional notes about what you observed.
  3. When you next document that area, return to the same Spot and add another image-backed Log.
  4. Review the Logs in date order or export your recorded history when needed.

One Spot, dated Logs

The Spot Log keeps the history of one Spot in chronological order. Open a Log to revisit its saved image and notes.

Documentation, not diagnosis

Derma.Log organizes what you record. It does not diagnose eczema, assess severity, identify triggers or recommend treatment. A photo history does not replace medical care. For medical concerns, contact a qualified medical professional; for urgent symptoms, seek local urgent or emergency care.
