# Derma.Log App Store Presence

## Boundary

The Derma.Log app lives in the private app repository/project. This skill handles public App Store material only. Do not add app implementation details, private repository paths, backend provider details, database details, private legal source files, or unreleased internal plans to public copy.

## Product Positioning

Present Derma.Log as a calm, structured skin observation and record-keeping app.

Allowed themes:

- image-based skin record organization
- spot histories and timelines
- private records by default
- export and backup awareness
- preparation for discussions with qualified professionals
- privacy, dignity, and user control

Disallowed themes:

- diagnosis, screening, triage, or treatment
- detecting, ruling out, predicting, or grading cancer or disease
- AI scanner claims
- emergency-care substitution
- clinician-created medical record claims unless a clinician separately reviews and adopts records
- guarantees about restore behavior beyond current app behavior

## App Store Copy Workflow

1. Identify the requested App Store surface: name, subtitle, promotional text, description, keywords, screenshot captions, review notes, privacy explanation, or support/legal linkage.
2. Keep wording concise, public-facing, and medically conservative.
3. Prefer concrete product behavior over claims of clinical value.
4. Link public legal/support pages from this repository when needed.
5. Use screenshots and assets only when they are already public-approved or explicitly supplied for App Store use.

## Review Before Delivery

Check that the result:

- does not imply diagnosis, cancer detection, or medical certainty
- does not disclose private implementation details
- matches public website/legal wording
- avoids fear, hype, and unsupported health benefit claims
- remains suitable for App Store review and user-facing publication
