Combine today’s technology and high-resolution cameras with old-school bookkeeping, and you get Derma.Log.

A Photo logbook for skin changes: moles, rashes, eczema patches, psoriasis flare-ups, bug bites, red spots, healing marks, or anything you want to keep an eye on.

Take a photo. Add a note. Come back later and add another, build a timeline, instead of having to scroll through 1000s of images in your Photo library.

Use it to:
- keep photos grouped by the same spot or skin concern
- see what happened, when, and where
- record simple observations and notes
- export a clean record when you want to prepare for an appointment
- keep sensitive skin records handled with care

Why Derma.Log is unique?
Because its designed for real usage, backed by real needs, used by the author.

FEATURES

• Create image-based skin histories
• Track progression with chronological logs
• Organize observations by body area
• Review spot development through simple timelines
• Create reminders for follow-ups, appointments, treatments and more
• Protect sensitive spots with a privacy shield
• Export reports in PDF, CSV, and JSON formats
• Create personal backup files
• Secure account-based synchronization

PRIVACY FIRST

Your history belongs to you!

Derma.Log is designed around private, user-controlled records, and preserve careful medical-language boundaries. The app helps organize and review skin observations, but does not provide medical diagnoses or emergency evaluation.

- No advertising.
- No tracking across apps.
- No behavioral profiling.

Only the account information required to operate the service is used.
That means, Your history remains private and under your control.

IMPORTANT

Derma.Log is an observation and documentation tool only and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

If you are concerned about a skin change or medical condition, contact a qualified healthcare professional.
